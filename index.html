How I work

  • 01You describe the task. I come back with a fixed price and a delivery date before any work starts.
  • 02Partial payment up front, the rest on delivery. Full payment up front on small tasks.
  • 03For new systems, you get the work as a file you import yourself. For fixes in an existing game, I'll need a copy of the place or temporary Team Create access.
  • 04One round of revisions included, plus 7 days of bug fixes on anything I wrote.
  • 05If I'm going to be slower than agreed, you hear it from me first, not after the deadline.
